About Me

I obtained my Ph.D. degree from Tsinghua in 2024, advised by Professor Youyou Lu and Jiwu Shu, with guidance from Qing Wang. My main research interests include programmable network, RDMA, and transaction processing. Before that, I received my B.S. degree from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) in 2019.
